Frequently asked

Why does the app need full photo library access?

To build the private on-device search index. Your photos are read locally and never transmitted anywhere — the app has no networking capability.

Why do results appear gradually at first?

On first launch MomentFind organizes your library in the background. Search works immediately on the portion already organized, and coverage grows until complete. This runs automatically and pauses when your phone is hot or the battery is low.

How do I delete the search index?

Settings → Organizing → Reindex all photos, or simply delete the app. The index exists only on your device.

A search returns no results

Try a different description, in any language. The on-device model understands 100+ languages, including mixed ones.
