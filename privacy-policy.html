Privacy Policy

No network. No account. No data collection.

Last updated: October 13, 2026

The short version

MomentFind works entirely on your device. Your photos and videos never leave it. The app has no networking capability at all — it cannot send your data anywhere even if it wanted to.

What the app does

MomentFind organizes your photo and video library into a private, on-device search index using a compact AI model that ships inside the app. Search, playback, and every other feature run locally on your iPhone.

What we collect

  • Nothing. No analytics, no identifiers, no tracking, no advertising.
  • The app makes zero network requests. You can verify this with any network monitor.

Photo library access

MomentFind requests full access to your photo library for one purpose: to read your photos and videos and build the local search index. The index is stored only on your device and can be erased at any time from the app's settings (or by deleting the app).

Children

The app contains no mature content and does not collect any data, including from children.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, the updated version will be posted on this page. Given the app's design, material changes are not expected.
